Official Exam Image Specifications Reference

Verified guidelines for candidate photograph and signature uploads across major national and state recruitment portals.

Exam Board Type Dimensions Size Bounds Mandatory Rules
TNPSC (Group 1, 2, 4) Photograph 3.5 × 4.5 cm 20 KB – 50 KB White background; Full name and Date of Photo (DOP) printed at bottom
TNPSC Signature 6.0 × 2.0 cm 10 KB – 20 KB Blue/Black ink on clean white paper; strict horizontal ratio

SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS) Photograph 3.5 × 4.5 cm 20 KB – 50 KB Clear front view, both ears visible; no caps or dark glasses
SSC Signature 4.0 × 2.0 cm 10 KB – 20 KB Black ink preferred; illegible or blurry crops are disqualified
